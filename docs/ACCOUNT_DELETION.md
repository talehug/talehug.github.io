# Delete your TaleHUG account or data

You can delete your TaleHUG account, or only some of your data, yourself, inside the app. No email is needed.

## Delete your whole account (in the app)

1. Open TaleHUG and go to the **Grown-up area** (hold the button for 3 seconds, or type the number shown in words).
2. Open **Settings**, then **Account and privacy**.
3. Tap **Delete my account**, then **Yes, delete everything**.

## Delete only some data (in the app)

- **A family voice and the stories read in it:** Grown-up area → **Family voices** → tap the voice → **Delete this voice**. Nothing else is removed.
- **Downloaded stories:** Grown-up area → **Downloads** → **Delete**. They are only on your phone.

## What is deleted

Deleting your account removes **everything straight away**: your family and account record, subscription link, favourites and settings, every family voice and its recordings, the voice models held by our voice provider, voice photos, and all converted stories. **It cannot be undone and nothing can be recovered**, so you would need to sign up again and start afresh.

## What is kept

- Limited records we must keep for legal or accounting reasons, if any apply.
- Purchases are handled by Google Play. Deleting your TaleHUG account does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.
- Encrypted database backups held by our hosting provider may briefly contain account rows until they expire (up to **7 days**). They are not used to restore deleted accounts. Stored files (recordings, audio, photos) are not in those backups.

## Can't open the app?

Email **talehug.stories@gmail.com** from the address you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete your account and data and confirm by email.
